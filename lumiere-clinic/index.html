Meet the Specialist

Dr. Noura Haddad

Dermatology & Aesthetic Skin Care

Dr. Noura Haddad focuses on evidence-based skin care, subtle aesthetic results, and personalized treatment planning for long-term skin health. She trained in dermatology and aesthetic medicine across Jordan and Europe, bringing an international perspective to her Amman-based practice.

Her philosophy is simple: beautiful skin begins with understanding. She takes the time to truly listen to each patient, connecting underlying skin biology with the right clinical approach — and translating that into a plan that fits your lifestyle, your skin, and your expectations.
